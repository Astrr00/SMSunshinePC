# Roadmap

Goal: a fully playable native Windows x86-64 port of Super Mario Sunshine (JP, GMSJ01), built from `sms/` on Aurora.
Acceptance requires normal controller-input play, reliable saves, all original content and a reproducible Windows package.
Dev starts, teleporting, automatic boss aiming and cheats provide diagnostic evidence only. PAL/US support and 100% byte matching are separate work.

Reviewed against recorded evidence on **2026-10-19**; no new gameplay certification in this documentation update.
The [playability matrix](sunshine-pc/docs/playability-matrix.tsv) has **16 passed-bounded, 8 open and 1 external-check-required** rows. These counts are not a completion percentage.

## Where we are

- Normal airport/Gatekeeper/Shadow Mario progression passes. Gatekeeper already has two consecutive `/O2` passes (`gk14`, `gk15`); the old sweep is complete.
- Petey, Gooper Blooper and Gelato's sandcastle have normal completion/save and independent cold-reload evidence. The earned save holds six Shines: IDs0/1/10/20/86/117. [Routes, save provenance and limits](sunshine-pc/docs/normal-world-routes.md).
- All 91 present later-world diagnostic load probes pass on their recorded Strict build. They do not certify normal unlocking, missions or collectibles. [Coverage](sunshine-pc/docs/world-load-coverage.tsv).
- Replay-row selection, stick-rotation flags, pickup fields, coaster-mode lookup, slide direction and water-gauge initialization have bounded fixes and regressions. [Evidence log](sunshine-pc/docs/status.md). None closes the broad PC-CORE row.
- The owner has verified the first two Shines on physical hardware, including picture, sound and controller input. Later scenes and a full playthrough remain open.
- v0.1.0 is a draft. Historical packages are not a current release candidate. [Release checklist](sunshine-pc/docs/release-checklist.md).

## Execution limits

- Run focused regressions for the changed behavior. Repeat or broaden only after a relevant change, failure or unresolved concern; do not rerun the completed `/O2` sweep or 91-scenario load batch by default.
- The coaster combat path, tiny-speed slide branch and audible refill behavior retain the specific limits in their evidence documents. A missing native observation is not itself a newly proven defect.
- No `/Od` comparison exists. Attribute a failure to undefined behavior only after evidence; an empty `crash.log` is normal.
- Long runs stay in the background with summary scripts and short updates. Check live free space on `C:` first; use the profile-preservation rules below.
- Preserve `sunshine-pc/docs/decomp-matching-worklist.tsv` and Aurora submodule changes unstaged. No HANDOFF.md or README status table.
